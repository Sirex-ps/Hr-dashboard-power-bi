# Hr-dashboard-power-bi

Developed an interactive HR dashboard in Power BI to provide a comprehensive overview of workforce metrics. The dashboard visualizes key HR data such as:
	•	Headcount by Job Title and Gender: Displays workforce distribution across roles and gender breakdown via bar and pie charts.
	•	Average Salary and Leave Balances: Summarizes key HR KPIs like average salary, average leave balance, and employees with leave balance over 20 days.
	•	Age Distribution: Uses age bins segmented by gender to analyze workforce demographics.
	•	Education vs. Salary Analysis: Scatter plot visualizing the relationship between employee qualifications, salary levels, and qualification IDs.

This dashboard enables HR teams to monitor workforce structure, identify gaps, and support strategic workforce planning through data-driven insights.
